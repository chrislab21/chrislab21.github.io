Fetch - Branding Exercise

After Effects

For this project I wanted to flex my 2D animation muscles in a style that has been popping up a lot lately. This overall style was something I workshopped in Illustrator and then created some assets for myself to animate a short little snippet in After Effects. I used a random word generator to pick a word (fetch) and ran with the ideas from there! I'm proud of how this turned out and I think it showcases my ability to design and animate assets and build up a brand voice.
